Optipixa

The right size, every time.

Optipixa shrinks photos to the exact size an online form asks for, like “350×450 px, under 50 KB”, right on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Set a maximum file size in KB and get the best quality that fits
  • Presets for passport photos, signatures and A4 documents
  • Crop, rotate and straighten before compressing
  • Compress up to 50 photos at once
  • Originals are never changed; location data is removed from results
  • No ads, no account, no tracking, works fully offline

Quick help

How do I get a photo under a size limit?
Pick a photo, then enter the limit in Max file size. Optipixa finds the best quality that fits and shows the result size before you save.
Why is my passport photo cropped?
Presets fill their exact size so faces are never stretched. Tap Crop and rotate to choose which part to keep.
Where are my compressed photos?
Tap Save to Photos. They appear in your gallery as new copies; your originals stay untouched.
Why is the result a JPEG?
JPEG is the format almost every form accepts and gives the smallest files for photos.
Does it need the internet?
No. On Android the app doesn’t even have internet permission. Try it in airplane mode.
